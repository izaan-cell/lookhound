You know the file you want. You just can't remember what you called it.

With Lookhound, you don't have to. Type what you remember, like *"that PDF about the school
trip"*, *"the blue logo"* or *"mum's banana bread recipe"*, and Lookhound finds it, even if
the file name has nothing to do with what you typed.

## What it can do

- **Search in your own words.** Describe a photo, a document, some code or an app the way
  you'd describe it to a friend.
- **Search with a picture.** Drop an image onto the window to find ones that look like it,
  or the app with a matching icon.
- **One search box for everything.** Photos, documents, code and apps, with simple filters
  when you only want one kind.
- **Go straight there.** Click a result and it opens in Finder or File Explorer, already
  selected.
- **Always up to date.** Lookhound quietly watches your folders, so a file you saved a moment
  ago is already searchable.

## Made to look after you

Lookhound greets you by name, keeps your recent searches one tap away, and shows you what's
new on your computer. It never nags, never upsells, and never asks you to sign up.

## Your files stay yours

Everything happens on your own computer. Lookhound never uploads your files or your
searches. There are no accounts, no subscriptions, no ads and no tracking. Read the full
[Privacy Policy](https://lookhound.spools.studio/privacy).

## Getting started

1. [Download Lookhound](https://lookhound.spools.studio/download) for your Mac or PC.
2. Open it. The first time, it takes a few minutes to settle in and get to know your files.
3. Type what you're looking for.

Lookhound looks in your Desktop, Documents, Downloads and Pictures folders, plus your apps.
Click **Folders** at the bottom of the window to choose different places.

**If your computer warns you the first time you open it:** on a Mac, go to System Settings,
then Privacy & Security, and click **Open Anyway**. On Windows, click **More info**, then
**Run anyway**.
